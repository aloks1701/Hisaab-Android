    What happens when you pay

    Five steps, none of them yours

    The payment already produced a notification. Everything after that is the app's job, and all of it runs on the device.

    01

    Listen

    A notification listener sees the payment arrive from a UPI app, a bank app, or the bank's SMS.

    02

    Parse

    Amount, direction and counterparty are pulled out. Anything without all three is dropped.

    03

    Categorise

    Zomato becomes खाना, indianoil becomes पेट्रोल. Weak guesses are flagged, not hidden.

    04

    De-duplicate

    Your bank and your UPI app both announce the same payment. It is recorded once.

    05

    Write

    The row lands in the ledger with the day's running total, in integer paise. Never floating point.

    The privacy claim, checkable

    It cannot phone home

    Every permission Hisaab declares, merged across all its libraries

    There is no INTERNET permission. Not "we promise not to" — the operating system will not let the app open a socket. Unzip the APK and check. Notification access is the one thing it asks for, granted from a system settings screen and revocable there.
